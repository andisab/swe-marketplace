Retrieval vocabulary

Six words for the rest of the talk

Retrieval finds the few passages a model needs and puts them in its context, so it answers from your documents instead of its memory.
ChunkA passage cut from a document, a few hundred tokens long.
EmbeddingA vector for a chunk; nearby vectors mean similar text.
top-kHow many chunks the retriever returns per question.
recall@kShare of questions whose answer is in the top k at all.
RerankerA second model that reads each question and chunk together and reorders them.
Hybrid searchKeyword and vector results merged before reranking.

Rules

Measure recall@k before tuning the prompt; a missing chunk can't be prompted back.

Keep k small after reranking; every extra chunk is noise the model must read.

Chunk on the document's own structure first, length second.
