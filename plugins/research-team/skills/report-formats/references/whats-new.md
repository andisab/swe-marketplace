# What's new

Covers recent changes to one product, standard, library or field for a reader who already knows it. Shorter than the other types: 300 to 600 words.

## Sections

1. **Title with the period covered,** such as "What's new in Gemini CLI, July to October 2026".
2. **Summary.** The three changes that most affect a user, in one paragraph.
3. **Changes, newest first.** One short section per change: what changed, the version or date, who it affects, and what to do about it. Cite the release note or announcement.
4. **Deprecations and removals.** Anything that stops working, with its date.
5. **Mentioned without detail.** Changes the sources name but don't explain. The reader can check these themselves.
6. **Sources.**

## Example skeleton (Markdown profile)

```markdown
# What's new in Gemini CLI, July to October 2026

Summary paragraph.

## 0.65: headless runs need a trusted folder (October 2026)
What changed, who it affects, what to do.
```
