# Technical rundown

A deep dive on one tool, library, framework or platform for an engineer who wants to become productive with it fast. Longer than the other types: 800 to 1,500 words.

## Sections

1. Title with the tool's name and the version covered.
2. Overview. Who makes it and since when, what it does, how it works at a high level in one paragraph, and how it differs from the two or three tools a reader would compare it with.
3. Key resources. The official site, documentation, repository and community forum, as links.
4. Strengths and limitations. Three to five of each, every one specific and cited: a benchmark, a missing feature, a pricing fact.
5. Getting started. Install, the first command or call, and the commands a daily user needs, each as `` `command` `` followed by one line on what it does.
6. Sections the tool calls for, such as language support, pricing and licensing, deployment options (cloud, on-premise, package manager), API availability, hardware requirements and integrations. Include only those the research notes cover.
7. Adoption. Release cadence, stars or downloads with their date, and who is known to use it.
8. Sources.
