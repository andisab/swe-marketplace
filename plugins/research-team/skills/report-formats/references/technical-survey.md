# Technical survey

Compares several tools, services or approaches in one category so a reader can shortlist. Best for 3 to 8 candidates; beyond that, group them.

## Sections

1. **Title and one-paragraph summary.** What was compared, on which criteria, and the finding that matters most.
2. **Comparison table.** One row per candidate, one column per criterion the user cares about, such as license, pricing model, hosting, language support, last release. Put a date in the table caption, because these facts change.
3. **One section per candidate.** Say what it is and who makes it, where it's strongest, what it lacks, and the pricing or licensing that matters, in two to four paragraphs with a citation for each fact.
4. **How to choose.** Map common situations to candidates: "if you need X, pick A". Say which criteria drove each mapping.
5. **Open questions.** Facts the research couldn't settle, with what would settle them.
6. **Sources.**
