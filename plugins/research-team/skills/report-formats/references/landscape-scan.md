# Landscape scan

Maps a field for a reader who is new to it: the problem, who works on it, the main approaches, and where it's going. The default type when no other fits.

## Sections

1. **Title and summary.** The field in one paragraph, and the two or three findings that matter most.
2. **The problem.** What the field is trying to do and why it's hard, in everyday words. Define each technical term once.
3. **Main approaches.** One subsection per approach or camp: how it works, who uses it, what it's good at and where it breaks. Cite each claim.
4. **Who's active.** Companies, projects, labs or standards bodies, with what each is known for and a recent dated fact about each.
5. **Direction.** Trends the sources agree on, and the disagreements, with both sides cited.
6. **Where to read next.** Three to five sources for a reader going deeper, with one line each on what they cover.
7. **Sources.**
